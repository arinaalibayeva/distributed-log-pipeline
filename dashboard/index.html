How to read this
  • Each row is one service's log (a partition), copied to all three brokers.
  • The blue cell is the leader: producers write to it and it copies lines to the others. It glows for a few seconds after a takeover.
  • In sync copies have everything; amber copies are catching up.
  • A line is committed (safe) once 2 of 3 brokers have it; faded lines aren't yet. Producers get their ack only then; until then a line is waiting and gets resent.
  • Kill crashes a broker (its log is lost); Restart brings it back empty. Cut off breaks its links to the other brokers until Reconnect.
  • Stopped: the process is dead. Unreachable: running but not answering.
  • The correctness check compares committed message IDs and their order among available copies that are close to the leader. It does not compare message contents or prove that every acknowledged line survived.
  • Error burst makes a producer send 5 errors at once, so the alert consumer raises an alert.
