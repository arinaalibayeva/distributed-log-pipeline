Network Lose messages between brokers:
Each cell is one broker's copy of a partition: length, commit index (highest committed offset) and its last lines. The leader's cell has a blue border, copies behind the leader are amber, and faded lines aren't committed yet. Kill stops a broker at once and loses its log; Restart brings it back empty. Cut off loses every message between that broker and the others (producers can still reach it), and the drop buttons lose that share of all messages between brokers. Heal network undoes both.
