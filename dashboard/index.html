How to read this

  • Each row is one service's log (a partition), copied to all three brokers.
  • The blue cell is the leader: producers write to it and it copies lines to the others. It glows for a few seconds after a takeover.
  • In sync copies have everything; amber copies are catching up.
  • A line is committed (safe) once 2 of 3 brokers have it; faded lines aren't yet.
  • Kill crashes a broker (its log is lost); Restart brings it back empty. Cut off breaks its links to the other brokers until Reconnect.
  • Stopped: the process is dead. Unreachable: running but not answering.
